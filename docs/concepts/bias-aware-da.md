# Bias-aware data assimilation

Real-time data assimilation combines quick estimates from a numerical model with
experimental data. Low-order models are, by construction, **biased**: they rely on
simplifications, so their predictions differ from the true physics by more than
aleatoric noise. Bias-aware data assimilation estimates that model bias on the fly and
corrects for it during the assimilation.

## Definitions

The model bias is the expected difference between the true observable and the model
observable,

$$
\mathbf{b} = \mathbf{d}^{\dagger} - \mathbb{E}(\mathbf{q}),
$$

so the bias-corrected model observable is

$$
\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{q} + \mathbf{b} + \boldsymbol{\epsilon}_q .
$$

The bias is an *unknown unknown* — it may depend on the state, the environment, or
time. To infer it we analyse the **innovations**

$$
\mathbf{i} = \mathbf{d} - \mathbf{q}, \qquad \mathbb{E}(\mathbf{i}) = \mathbf{b},
$$

i.e., the expected innovation is the model bias. In `romda`, a
[`Bias`][romda.bias_estimators.bias.Bias] estimator forecasts the bias between
analyses and is re-anchored to the analysis innovation
$\mathbf{i}^a = \mathbf{d} - \langle \mathbf{y}^a \rangle$ after each assimilation.

## The regularized bias-aware EnKF (r-EnKF)

The r-EnKF ([Nóvoa, Racca & Magri, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2023.116502))
minimizes a cost function with three norms — the ensemble spread, the distance between
the bias-corrected estimate and the data, and the bias norm weighted by a
user-defined regularization factor $\gamma \ge 0$:

!!! warning "Erratum to the published paper"
    Equations (15)–(16) of the published CMAME paper contain small typos in the
    transposes of the Jacobian terms. The equations below — and the
    [`rBA_EnKF`][romda.data_assimilation.rBA_EnKF] implementation — follow the
    **corrected** formulation of the
    [2024 erratum](../2023_CMAME_Erratum.pdf). The published and corrected forms
    coincide for a single observation ($N_q = 1$), where the Jacobian is a scalar;
    no conclusion of the paper is affected.

$$
\mathcal{J}(\boldsymbol{\psi}_j) =
\left\| \boldsymbol{\psi}_j - \boldsymbol{\psi}_j^\mathrm{f} \right\|^2_{\mathbf{C}^{\mathrm{f}^{-1}}_{\psi\psi}} +
\left\| \mathbf{y}_j - \mathbf{d}_j \right\|^2_{\mathbf{C}^{-1}_{dd}} +
\gamma \left\| \mathbf{b}_j \right\|^2_{\mathbf{C}^{-1}_{bb}} .
$$

The analysis update is

$$
\boldsymbol{\psi}_j^\mathrm{a} = \boldsymbol{\psi}_j^\mathrm{f} +
\mathbf{K}_\mathrm{r}
\left[ (\mathbb{I} + \mathbf{J})^\mathrm{T} (\mathbf{d}_j - \mathbf{y}_j^\mathrm{f})
- \gamma\, \mathbf{C}_{dd}\mathbf{C}^{-1}_{bb} \mathbf{J}^\mathrm{T} \mathbf{b}^\mathrm{f} \right],
$$

with the regularized Kalman gain

$$
\mathbf{K}_\mathrm{r} = \mathbf{C}_{\psi\psi}^\mathrm{f} \mathbf{M}^\mathrm{T}
\left[ \mathbf{C}_{dd}
+ (\mathbb{I}+\mathbf{J})^\mathrm{T}(\mathbb{I}+\mathbf{J})\, \mathbf{M}\mathbf{C}_{\psi\psi}^\mathrm{f}\mathbf{M}^\mathrm{T}
+ \gamma\, \mathbf{C}_{dd}\mathbf{C}^{-1}_{bb} \mathbf{J}^\mathrm{T}\mathbf{J}\, \mathbf{M}\mathbf{C}_{\psi\psi}^\mathrm{f}\mathbf{M}^\mathrm{T}
\right]^{-1},
$$

where $\mathbf{J} = \mathrm{d}\mathbf{b}/\mathrm{d}(\mathbf{M}\boldsymbol{\psi})$ is
the Jacobian of the bias estimator, supplied by
[`Bias.state_derivative`][romda.bias_estimators.bias.Bias.state_derivative]. In the
unbiased limit ($\mathbf{b}=\mathbf{0}$, $\mathbf{J}=\mathbf{0}$) the r-EnKF reduces to
the stochastic EnKF.

This simplified form assumes uncorrelated observations
($\mathbf{C}_{dd} = \sigma_d \mathbb{I}$); the general form is derived in the
[erratum](../2023_CMAME_Erratum.pdf).

## Bias estimators

| Estimator | Forecast model | Jacobian $\mathbf{J}$ | Use case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`NoBias`][romda.bias_estimators.NoBias] | $b \equiv 0$ | $\mathbf{0}$ | unbiased limit / sanity checks |
| [`ConstantBias`][romda.bias_estimators.ConstantBias] | $\dot b = 0$ (persistent) | $\mathbf{0}$ | slowly-varying biases |
| [`DriftLinearBias`][romda.bias_estimators.DriftLinearBias] | $\dot b = v + A b$ | $A$ | drifting / decaying biases |
| [`ESN_bias`][romda.bias_estimators.ESN_bias] | echo state network | from the reservoir | state-dependent, nonlinear biases |

The ESN estimator is trained offline on synthetic innovation data generated by
perturbing the low-order model (see
[`create_bias_training_dataset`][romda.bias_estimators.aux.create_bias_training_dataset])
and runs in closed loop between analyses, with one open-loop step at each analysis.

For a hands-on walk-through, see the tutorial
[*13 — bias-aware DA intro*](https://github.com/andreanovoa/real-time-bias-aware-DA/blob/main/scripts/tutorials/1_Introtuction_to_real-time_DA/13_bias-aware-DA-intro.ipynb).
